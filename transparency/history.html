Evidence, not a security guarantee

Reports are observations at a named Ethereum block. Runtime and parameter conformity do not establish formal verification or a third-party security audit.

Eligibility ≠ Distribution. A releasable balance is not an automatic distribution. Treasury assets remain governed by the corresponding Safe; its owners, threshold and extensions can change under Safe rules.

49M production funding + historical liquidity + Source residual is historical accounting, not the sum of current Vault balances. H9’s 23 permanently ineligible base units are inside H9’s allocation. They are separate from the 23,854,400-base-unit Funding Source residual.

SHA-256 checks detect changes to the indexed report bytes. They do not independently authenticate the website operator or prove that an RPC supplied complete chain history. Corrections append a new record with a supersedes reference.

The website is a reviewed projection of the canonical repository archive. No private signer identities or custody information are collected or displayed.
