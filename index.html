Eligibility ≠ Distribution ≠ Circulation ≠ Sale

All scheduled amounts represent maximum release eligibility. Reaching an eligibility date does not automatically distribute tokens. Actual release may be zero, partial, or up to the currently available eligible amount. Unused eligibility can accumulate within the contract limits. No automatic distribution or mandatory catch-up occurs.

Zero release means no release transaction. A successful release(uint256 amount) requires a positive amount. Treasury use remains subject to the allocation’s purpose and genuine project needs.

How release works

release(uint256 amount) requires an explicit Ethereum transaction. It does not happen automatically.

  • Any address can call it. The caller cannot choose the destination and does not receive tokens merely by calling.
  • Tokens always go to the fixed Treasury for that Vault.
  • The amount must be positive and cannot exceed either remaining eligibility (releasable) or the actual Vault balance.
  • availableToReleaseNow is the smaller of those two limits, not an amount already released.
  • State is updated before transfer; a transfer failure reverts the transaction.

Immutable destination. Safe-managed Treasury.

Each releasable Vault has an immutable Treasury address. Treasury funds are managed by the corresponding Safe. Safe signer and threshold configuration is operational governance, not the same as the immutable Vault Treasury address. Signers, threshold, modules, guard and fallback handler can change under valid Safe rules; changes require separate review and recording.

Treasury spending and distribution are separate from release into the Treasury. Unused resources may remain in the Vault or Treasury; reaching eligibility does not require a sale or forced distribution.

Historical accounting: two different residuals

The Funding Source accounting residual is 23,854,400 base units (0.0000000000238544 MDC), the difference between the nominal 5M Batch 1 allowance and the actual historical LP contribution. The Production Registry records it at the Funding Source in snapshot S1. It is not a new allocation and is not added to H7 or another Vault.

H9’s 23 permanently ineligible base units are already inside H9’s allocation and the 49M funding total. They are completely separate from the Source residual.

49,000,000 MDC production funding + 4,999,999.9999999999761456 MDC historical Batch 1 + 0.0000000000238544 MDC Source residual = 54,000,000 MDC. This is historical funding reconciliation, not a formula for the sum of future current Vault balances.

Vision

A Simpler Digital Experience

MDC aims to explore ways to make digital value easier to use in everyday life.

Today, blockchain can still feel complicated. Wallets, networks, and gas fees can create friction for everyday users.

The long-term MDC vision is to develop tools and applications that simplify the experience, so people can focus on sending, receiving, and managing MDC without needing to understand every technical layer behind it.

Future development may explore technologies that reduce complexity around transaction fees and network configuration.
